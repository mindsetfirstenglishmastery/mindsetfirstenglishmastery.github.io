Who I work with

This is for you if any of this sounds familiar.

  • You understand almost everything you read and hear, but speaking feels much harder than it should.
  • You rehearse a sentence in your head, and by the time you are ready the moment has passed.
  • You go blank in meetings, on calls, or when someone asks you a direct question.
  • You have studied English for years and feel stuck at the same level.
  • You are preparing for a Cambridge exam and the speaking part worries you most.

Why mindset comes first

I have taught English for more than ten years, and I speak four languages myself. I know the feeling of having the words and still not being able to say them. As a certified mindset coach, I work on what blocks the speaking before we drill the speaking. That order matters, because practice does not help much while the fear is still running the show.
